Snapchat Planets: Complete Guide to Planet Order, Meanings, and Friend Solar System

Introduction

Snapchat Planets is a fun feature of Snapchat+ that helps users understand their friendship rankings through a unique planetary system. Also known as the Friend Solar System, this feature represents your closest friends as planets revolving around the Sun. Each planet has a specific position and meaning, showing how frequently you interact with another person on Snapchat.

The Snapchat Planets feature makes friendships more interesting by turning your Best Friends list into a visual solar system. Depending on your position in someone's Best Friends list, your Bitmoji appears on a different planet. The closer the planet is to the Sun, the higher your friendship ranking.

In this guide, we will explore Snapchat Planets, their complete order, meanings, and how the Friend Solar System works.

What Are Snapchat Planets?

Snapchat Planets is a feature available to Snapchat+ subscribers that represents friendship rankings using a solar system. In this system, your friend acts as the Sun, while your Bitmoji appears on a planet based on your friendship position.

Snapchat uses eight different planets to represent the top eight friends. These positions are based on interactions between users, such as sending snaps and chatting with each other.

For example, if your Bitmoji appears on Mercury, you hold the first position in that person's Best Friends list. If it appears on Venus, you are in the second position.

This feature provides a visual and entertaining way to understand friendships without displaying a traditional ranking list.

Meaning of All Eight Snapchat Planets

Every planet in Snapchat has a different meaning. Let's explore what each planet represents and how it reflects your friendship ranking.

1. Mercury – First Best Friend

Mercury is the first planet in Snapchat Planets and represents the number-one position in someone's Best Friends list. If your Bitmoji appears on Mercury, it means you are that person's closest friend on Snapchat according to their interactions.

Mercury is usually represented by a reddish or pink planet surrounded by small hearts. Its appearance makes it one of the easiest planets to recognize.

2. Venus – Second Best Friend

Venus represents the second-closest Best Friend in Snapchat's Friend Solar System. If your Bitmoji appears on Venus, you hold the second position in that person's friendship ranking.

The planet typically features a light yellow or beige appearance with colorful hearts around it. It represents a strong friendship and frequent communication.

3. Earth – Third Best Friend

Earth is the third planet in Snapchat Planets. It represents the person ranked third in someone's Best Friends list based on Snapchat interactions.

Earth has a blue and green appearance, similar to our real planet. It may also feature a Moon and small stars, making its design visually interesting.

4. Mars – Fourth Best Friend

Mars represents the fourth position in the Snapchat friendship ranking. If your Bitmoji appears on Mars, you are among that person's top four friends.

Mars is generally shown as a reddish-orange planet with small stars and other decorative elements. It represents another important position in the Friend Solar System.

5. Jupiter – Fifth Best Friend

Jupiter represents the fifth-closest Best Friend on Snapchat. If you see your Bitmoji on Jupiter, it means you hold the fifth position in that person's friendship ranking.

Jupiter is usually displayed as a large orange or reddish planet with distinctive stripes. Its design is inspired by the real Jupiter in our solar system.

6. Saturn – Sixth Best Friend

Saturn represents the sixth position in Snapchat Planets. It is recognized by its beautiful rings and golden or yellow appearance.

If your Bitmoji appears on Saturn, you are the sixth person in that user's Best Friends ranking. Its ringed design makes it one of the most recognizable planets.

7. Uranus – Seventh Best Friend

Uranus represents the seventh-closest Best Friend in the Friend Solar System. It usually appears as a light blue or cyan planet.

If your Bitmoji is on Uranus, you hold the seventh position in that person's top eight friends. It is another interesting planet in Snapchat's friendship ranking system.

8. Neptune – Eighth Best Friend

Neptune is the eighth and final planet in Snapchat Planets. It represents the eighth position in someone's Best Friends list.

Neptune generally has a deep blue or purple appearance with decorative stars. Although it is the final planet, it still represents a place among someone's top eight friends.

How Does the Snapchat Friend Solar System Work?

The Snapchat Friend Solar System works by using friendship rankings based on interactions between two Snapchat users.

When you visit a friend's profile, you may see a Best Friends or Friends badge if you are eligible for the feature.

If the Friend Solar System is enabled, you can tap the badge to view your planetary position. Your Bitmoji will appear on the planet representing your ranking in that person's Best Friends list.

The eight planets represent the top eight friendship positions, with Mercury being the closest and Neptune being the eighth.

Remember that your planet and your friend's planet may be different because friendship rankings are individual and depend on each person's interactions.

How to See Your Snapchat Planet?

If you want to check your position in someone's Snapchat Planets, follow these simple steps:

  1. Open the Snapchat application on your smartphone.
  2. Visit the profile of a friend.
  3. Look for the Best Friends or Friends badge.
  4. Tap the badge to explore the Friend Solar System.
  5. Check which planet contains your Bitmoji.

You may need a Snapchat+ subscription, and the feature must be enabled to access the solar system view.

Can Snapchat Planets Change?

Yes, your Snapchat Planet position can change over time. The rankings are based on interactions, so changes in communication patterns can affect your position.

For example, if you start sending more snaps and messages to someone, your ranking may improve. Similarly, if your interactions decrease, your position may change.

However, Snapchat does not provide a fixed ranking schedule. Your planet should not be treated as a permanent measure of friendship or someone's feelings.

Is Snapchat Planets Available for Everyone?

Snapchat Planets is a Snapchat+ feature rather than a standard feature available to every user. Snapchat+ subscribers can access additional features, including the Friend Solar System.

The feature may be turned off by default for some subscribers, so you may need to enable it through your Snapchat+ settings.

Availability and feature behavior can also vary depending on the app version and account settings.

Conclusion

Snapchat Planets makes friendship rankings more entertaining through a creative solar system. From Mercury, representing the first Best Friend, to Neptune, representing the eighth position, every planet has a unique meaning.

Understanding the Snapchat Planets order helps you discover your position in someone's Friend Solar System and makes it easier to understand how Snapchat represents friendships.

Whether your Bitmoji appears on Mercury, Venus, Earth, or Neptune, remember that these rankings reflect Snapchat interactions and should not define the value of a real friendship.
